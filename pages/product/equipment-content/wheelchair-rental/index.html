Which wheelchair should I rent?

The right wheelchair depends on how it will be used. A foldable manual wheelchair suits hospital visits and occasional use, especially if it needs to fit in a car. A commode wheelchair has a removable seat panel for toileting and is useful for patients who cannot walk to the bathroom. A motorised wheelchair suits people who want to move independently but cannot propel a manual chair.

Safety tips

  • Always apply the brakes before getting in or out.
  • Check that footrests are folded away during transfers.
  • When pushing a manual chair, use help and follow safe handling guidance on kerbs and slopes.

Who rents a wheelchair?

  • Patients recovering from fractures or joint surgery.
  • Stroke survivors and people with neurological conditions.
  • Older adults who tire easily or cannot walk long distances.
  • Families who need a wheelchair for a hospital visit or trip.

How to rent a wheelchair

Frequently asked questions

Can a foldable wheelchair fit in a car boot?

Many foldable manual wheelchairs fit in the boot of a hatchback or sedan. Tell us your car model and we can advise about the model available.

Is a commode wheelchair safe to use in the shower?

Check with us whether the specific model is suitable for wet areas. Not every commode chair is designed for shower use.

How long does a motorised wheelchair battery last?

Battery range depends on the model, load, terrain and battery condition. Charge and use it according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Can I rent a wheelchair for just a week?

Short-term rentals may be available. Contact us to confirm the minimum rental period and current availability.

Can I buy a wheelchair?

Yes. Wheelchairs are available for purchase as well as rent, subject to availability.

Which wheelchair is best after a stroke?

It depends on sitting balance and how much help the person needs. A physiotherapist can advise on a suitable chair and safe transfers.
