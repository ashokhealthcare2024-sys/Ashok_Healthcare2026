When is a suction machine needed at home?

A suction machine is used when a patient has secretions they cannot cough up or swallow, for example a patient with a tracheostomy, someone with weak swallowing after a stroke, or a patient receiving palliative or home ICU care. Suctioning should be done by a trained nurse or a family member who has been taught by a nurse, using the correct technique and pressure.

  • Bedridden or palliative care patients with excess secretions.

Frequently asked questions

Who can do suctioning at home?

A trained nurse, or a family member who has been properly taught by a nurse. Incorrect technique can injure the airway.

How often should the collection jar be emptied?

Empty it before it reaches the fill line and at least daily, then clean it as instructed.

Which catheter size is used?

The size depends on the patient and the tracheostomy tube, as advised by the nurse or doctor. Confirm available sizes and quantities with our team before rental.

Can I rent the machine without a nurse?

Yes, if a trained person will use it. We recommend nursing support for tracheostomy patients.
