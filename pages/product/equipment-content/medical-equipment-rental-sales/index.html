Should you rent or buy medical equipment?

Renting usually makes sense when equipment is needed for a short or uncertain period, for example during recovery from surgery or after a hospital discharge. Buying can be better for long-term needs, such as a permanent wheelchair or a CPAP machine used every night. Many families rent first, then decide whether to buy once the patient's needs are clear.

When a patient comes home from hospital, the room often needs to be prepared quickly: a bed that adjusts, oxygen, perhaps a suction machine or monitor. Ashok Surgical supplies this equipment from our base in Yeshwanthpur, so families can arrange it with one call and have it installed before the patient arrives.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a prescription to rent equipment?

Oxygen concentrators, BiPAP, CPAP and similar devices should be used only as prescribed by a doctor. Beds, wheelchairs and many other items do not need a prescription.

Is rented equipment clean?

Equipment is cleaned and checked before every rental, and single-patient items such as masks and cannulas are supplied new.

What if the equipment stops working?

Call our helpline. We will guide you over the phone and arrange repair or replacement if needed.

Can you also provide a nurse?

Yes. Our home nursing and home ICU teams can be arranged alongside the equipment.

Need equipment today?

Tell us what you need and where. We will confirm availability and the delivery time.
