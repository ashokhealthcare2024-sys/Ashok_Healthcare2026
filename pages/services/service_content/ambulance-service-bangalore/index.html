ALS and BLS ambulance information

A Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulance generally carries essential transport and first-aid equipment. An Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulance may carry additional equipment such as a cardiac monitor, defibrillator and ventilator, with a crew trained to provide advanced care during transport. Confirm the exact vehicle specification, equipment and crew credentials offered before describing Ashok Healthcare's service.

Planned transfers can include discharge from hospital, a move to a home ICU, transfer between hospitals, or travel for a test or appointment. Confirm the available transfer types and areas before presenting these as bookable services.
