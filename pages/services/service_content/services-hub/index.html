How do I know which home care service my family needs?

Start with what the patient needs help with, not with the service name. If the needs are clinical, such as injections, wounds, tubes or monitoring, you need a nurse. If the needs are daily living tasks, such as bathing, feeding and walking, a trained attendant is usually enough. If the goal is regaining movement, you need a physiotherapist. Our care coordinator can help you decide during a free assessment call.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide home care across all of Bangalore?

Our base is in Yeshwanthpur and most of our work is in North and West Bangalore. Some services may be available further away. Call us with the patient's address and we will confirm what we can arrange.

Can I book more than one service, for example a nurse and a hospital bed?

Yes. Many families combine nursing, physiotherapy and equipment. We coordinate them together so delivery, setup and the first nursing shift happen in the right order.

Do I need a doctor's prescription to start home care?

Clinical procedures such as IV fluids, injections and oxygen therapy must follow a doctor's prescription. Attendant care and most equipment rentals do not need a prescription, but sharing the discharge summary helps us plan safely.

How quickly can care start?

This depends on the service, the location and staff availability. Equipment and nursing can often be arranged the same day.

Is there an emergency number?

Our helpline, +91 78297 53538, is answered 24 hours a day. In a medical emergency, call 108 or go to the nearest hospital first.
